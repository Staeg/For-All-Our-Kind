For All Our Kind

Four otherwise friendly peoples agreed to a simulated war, to learn who would fare best in a real one and what each could learn from the others. Pick who you speak for.

How it plays
  • Every 20 seconds each base sends a wave down each of its three lanes: one to each neighbour and one through the free-for-all centre.
  • When enemy armies meet, a battle starts. Units phase in on the side they came from, in your formation, and fight it out. Reinforcements join running battles.
  • An army that reaches an undefended tower damages that player directly. Players have 10,000 HP. Last one standing wins.
  • Each kill earns 1 gold per tier. Spend 20 gold to add a unit to a lane's wave permanently, or 60 gold for a unit type's one-time upgrade (all lanes).
  • Formation sliders set where each unit type phases in: back of your quarter of the field, or front.
  • Space pauses. Click a battle on the map to watch it.
